Our Story

Made by a mom, for moms

Bath Bombs for Moms started in a small kitchen with a simple idea: moms give so much, they deserve something that gives back. Every bomb is handcrafted in small batches using skin-safe, cruelty-free ingredients — no sulfates, no artificial dyes, just pure relaxation. Whether you're treating yourself or gifting the mom in your life, we hope every fizz feels like a deep breath.
